## About

Modern digital systems run on signals.

My work examines the governance conditions under which signals are generated, admitted, interpreted, propagated, and relied upon across digital environments.

This includes structural governance questions relating to signal integrity, identity continuity, consent architecture, measurement conditions, and governance exposure.

The focus is upstream.

Not on data after it appears in dashboards, reports, attribution systems, or AI environments — but on the structural conditions that shape those outcomes earlier.

---

## Areas of Focus

- **Digital Signal Governance (DSG)** — Governance at the signal layer
- **Design-Time Governance** — Governance applied before operational deployment
- **Signal Integrity** — Reliability and consistency of signal meaning
- **Identity Continuity** — Structural consistency of entity representation
- **Consent Architecture** — Governance of consent conditions across systems
- **Measurement Governance** — Structural conditions shaping measurement validity
- **Governance Risk** — Exposure arising from weak upstream governance
